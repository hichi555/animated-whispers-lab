# ReadKidz public feature scan (2026-10-30)

Scope: official public product pages retrieved on 2026-10-30. A feature not described on these pages is not proof that ReadKidz lacks it.

- Homepage: https://www.readkidz.com/ — Describes picture books and animation; a review-first flow; reading level and story-length adaptation; editable story/page content; consistent character face, proportions and outfits; PDF and print outputs; classroom and creator audiences.
- Workflow: https://www.readkidz.com/how-it-works — Describes picture books, animation, kids’ music videos and comics; reviewing story plan, characters and scenes; editing individual lines and regenerating a single page/scene; human review before export.
- Picture book: https://www.readkidz.com/create/picture-book — Describes many illustration styles, page-level text/image revisions, reference photos, age bands, 8–30 pages, and 25 languages. It also describes adding a second-language translation beside existing page text.
- Animation maker: https://www.readkidz.com/ai-animation-maker — Describes choosing animation style, reading age and narration voice; character locks; narration and background music; several video aspect ratios; download/YouTube output.

Implementation implication: character consistency, style selection, comics, bilingual pages, narration and edit/regenerate workflows are existing ReadKidz claims, so they must not be marketed as unique differentiators. Potential CyliaTales differentiators should instead emphasize granular, transparent controls and connected workflow details, such as selecting the exact approved references per panel, retaining reusable per-character identity constraints, and voice delivery controls with generated-audio review. Phrase novelty narrowly; the published pages reviewed do not establish that ReadKidz lacks these capabilities.
