# CyliaTales capability map

CyliaTales uses the requested creative skills as production disciplines, not as decorative labels or fake provider buttons.

## Story and delivery

The read-aloud player uses one narration track per page, supports play/pause, seek, previous/next narration, keyboard page movement, remembered reading position, large type and reduced visual friction. Final delivery includes a print PDF with illustration and story text, a self-contained offline HTML reader with embedded page art and audio, and a ZIP source bundle containing manuscript, page art, narration and comic panels.

The HTML reader is built as an editable, scene-based delivery surface in the spirit of HTML video production: the story remains structured as page data, media is embedded deterministically, and playback advances through the narrative rather than presenting a static export.

## Visual continuity and motion

Character Story Video and Cinema Director principles drive Story World, cast locks, art-direction boards, camera motion fields and the Production Desk review gates. Motion Graphics and GSAP principles guide restrained transitions, page-turn movement, progress feedback and future title/scene sequences. Product showcase and cinematic-ad patterns are reserved for CyliaTales promotional assets and story trailers, not mixed into the children's reading experience.

Manim is reserved for classroom-ready explanatory animations and learning extensions where code-driven diagrams or step-by-step visual reasoning are appropriate. Excalidraw is reserved for editable story maps, classroom diagrams and production planning artifacts.

## Brand, documents and quality

Typst PDF principles inform the print-delivery standard when a future server-side book compositor is introduced. Illustrator playbooks inform portable SVG marks, icon systems and brand handoff rules. Tailwind v4 tokens remain the source of truth for the interface. Accessibility review is applied to reader controls, focus order, labels, contrast, touch targets and reduced-motion behavior.

Music Prompter and TTS Prompter principles govern future generated narration, bedtime beds and scene sound design. Photo restoration is reserved for user-provided reference material that needs identity-preserving cleanup before entering a Story World.

The product rule is simple: every capability must produce a real artifact, a real control, or a verifiable delivery state. Nothing is represented as completed when it is only a placeholder.
