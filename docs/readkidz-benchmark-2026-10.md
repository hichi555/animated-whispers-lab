# ReadKidz benchmark and CyliaTales response — October 2026

## Verified public surfaces

ReadKidz publicly presents a four-stage workflow: begin with an idea, shape the story, define the characters, then review and complete the book. Its picture-book creator emphasizes age-specific writing, page-level rewrite/redraw/recast, consistent characters, 25 languages, 8–30 pages depending on reading age, PDF export, 3D/pop-up reading, and optional hardcover printing. Source: https://www.readkidz.com/create/picture-book

Its animation creator presents a story-to-film workflow with scenes, narration, background music, camera movement, character consistency, and the ability to redo individual shots. Source: https://www.readkidz.com/create/animation

Its public pricing surface organizes Personal and Teams plans, monthly/yearly billing, credits, concurrency, stored project resources, artistic styles, voices, background music, watermarked free exports, print add-ons, and extra credits. Source: https://www.readkidz.com/pricing

The homepage also exposes multiple formats and discovery surfaces: picture books, animation, kids music videos, comics, graphic novels, storybooks, videos, tutorials, examples, and safety/trust information. Source: https://www.readkidz.com/

## CyliaTales response

CyliaTales should not copy ReadKidz visual identity or copy. It should compete through a more deliberate production system: one approved world layer feeding books, narrated reading, motion scenes, learning activities, posters, comics, and export delivery; per-page review controls; visible continuity state; and settings that make reading level, language, safety, privacy, accessibility, motion, and default art direction first-class choices.

The next product-quality pass implements a real Settings & Preferences center, improves the authenticated navigation and studio shell, preserves the original CyliaTales visual language, and makes the creation defaults persistent per browser while database-backed profile settings remain an extension point.
